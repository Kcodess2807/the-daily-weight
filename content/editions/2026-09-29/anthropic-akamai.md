Akamai disclosed in a press release and SEC filing on September 24 that Anthropic has committed $11.6 billion over seven years for compute on Akamai Cloud, with an option to add $9 billion more. It builds on an earlier $1.8 billion agreement and is the largest deal in Akamai's history. Bloomberg, which broke the news, rounded it to $12 billion.

The capacity is CPUs. Akamai expects about $5.5 billion of capital spending tied to the deal, more than six times its 2025 total, and $150 million to $300 million of revenue from Anthropic next year. Anthropic receives warrants for about 2% of Akamai's stock now, with up to about 3% more vesting at 1% for each $3 billion of expansion.
