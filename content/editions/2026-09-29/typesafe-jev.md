TypeSafe AI's Jev does not generate natural-language text. It takes unstructured state and returns typed, calibrated probabilistic decisions, sampled in parallel. The company calls it a "System One" model and says it is trained with Reinforcement Learning for Calibrated Decisions.

TypeSafe claims 70 to 500 ms latency and $0.042 per million input tokens with free output; early access opened September 15. The Register notes that the "hallucination-free" framing does not make a structured answer correct. Bloomberg reported on September 25 on its reception and investor interest.
