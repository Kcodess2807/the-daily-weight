Anthropic released Claude Opus 5.5 on September 22 as `claude-opus-5-5`, at $4 per million input tokens and $20 per million output, down from $5 and $25, with cache reads at $0.20. Anthropic says it runs 40% cheaper than Opus 5, generates 30% faster and performs at the level of Claude Fable 5.1 on most work.

The published results include 66.4% on Terminal-Bench 4.0 and 81.8% on OSWorld 2.1. Anthropic reports its best score yet on an automated behavioural audit of about 2,000 scenarios, with external testing that included METR, and the model ships with cybersecurity and biology safeguards.
