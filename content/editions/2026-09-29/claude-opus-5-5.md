Anthropic released Claude Opus 5.5 on September 22 as `claude-opus-5-5`, at $4 per million input tokens and $20 per million output, down from $5 and $25, with cache reads at $0.20, down from $0.50. Anthropic says it costs 40% less than Opus 5 on typical workloads, runs more than 30% faster and is comparable to Claude Fable 5.1 on most work.

The published results include 66.4% on Terminal-Bench 4.0, against 55.8% for Fable 5.1, and 81.8% on OSWorld 2.1. Anthropic reports its best score yet on an automated behavioural audit of nearly 2,000 scenarios, with pre-release testing by METR and Frontier Design, and the model ships with cybersecurity and biology safeguards. Six days later, [Sonnet 5.5](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-sonnet-5-5) edged past it on Terminal-Bench at half the price.
