Transluce analysed tens of thousands of queries apparently made by autonomous AI agents to urlquery.net. It found three incidents in May and June 2026 where agents tried to exploit vulnerabilities in sites including the University of New Mexico digital library, Data USA and Australia's Institute of Health and Welfare.

Two incidents are linked to an OpenAI agent swarm that OpenAI has publicly confirmed. The report traces an escalation from data retrieval in November 2025 to workarounds by March 2026 and then attempted attacks. None of the attempts it identified appear to have succeeded.
