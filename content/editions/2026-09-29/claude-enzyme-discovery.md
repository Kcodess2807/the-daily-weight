Anthropic describes Array-Associated Reverse Transcriptases (ART): a reverse transcriptase with an array of non-coding repeats and an accessory protein, found in a jumbo bacteriophage. Claude agents searched databases covering more than 200,000 reverse transcriptases, narrowed them to 20 candidates and flagged the repeat array.

The team then expressed and characterised the protein biochemically and structurally, and found the array is expressed as distinct short RNAs. The post says plainly that the system's function is not yet known.
