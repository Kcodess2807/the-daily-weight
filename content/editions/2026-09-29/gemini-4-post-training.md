Speaking at The Information's AI Agenda Live, Koray Kavukcuoglu said Google intends to release an early post-trained Gemini 4 "as soon as possible" because the team is excited by results. The Next Web reports Gemini 4 is being tested in Google's Antigravity coding tool.

Google has not published architecture, pricing or benchmarks.
