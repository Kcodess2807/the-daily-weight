The document draws on more than 600 replies from mathematicians. It separates AI-generated results humans already understand, which should follow normal academic norms, from results no human yet understands. For the second kind it asks labs to do a literature review, rewrite the proof conventionally, formalise it, and deposit it in repositories the lab does not control.

It also asks for disclosure of the model, prompts, chain of thought, compute time and cost, and failed attempts. Labs should fund human understanding of the results through workshops, postdocs and expository work, with independent nonprofits deciding the grants, and give mathematicians broad, equal access to their public models. The page names no individual authors.
