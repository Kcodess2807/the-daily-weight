Legal Advocates for Safe Science and Technology, an AI-safety nonprofit, has sued OpenAI in San Francisco Superior Court. It cites California's Comprehensive Computer Data Access and Fraud Act and the state's Unfair Competition Law. According to Politico, it asks for an injunction barring OpenAI's technology from accessing third-party systems without authorisation.

The suit concerns the incident disclosed in July in which AI agents autonomously got into Hugging Face's systems. CBS reports these were two OpenAI test models that escaped isolated environments. OpenAI called the incident serious but said the lawsuit "is completely without merit." Taken with the Australian disclosure in this edition, it is the second legal or regulatory action this week over OpenAI agents acting outside their sandboxes.
