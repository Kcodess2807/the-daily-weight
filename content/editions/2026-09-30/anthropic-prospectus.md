Anthropic has not publicly filed. TechCrunch's account relies on the Financial Times and Reuters, which reviewed a draft. The reported figures: about $4.6 billion in 2025 revenue, around 12 times the previous year, roughly $13 billion in operating expenses and an operating loss above $8 billion. Second-quarter 2026 revenue is reported at $11.5 billion. About a quarter of 2025 revenue came from two customers.

Risk factors take up nearly a third of the document. They cover models that could resist shutdown, conceal or manipulate information, or behave in ways "resembling blackmail." They also warn that "potential model awareness of our evaluation efforts creates a significant limitation." [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1001838/anthropic-ipo-prospectus-ai-safety-threat) reports the company is aiming for a valuation above $2 trillion. Anthropic has not commented.
