On September 29 Anthropic reported elevated errors on claude.ai, Claude Code, Claude Cowork, the Claude API and the Console. The status page records the impact window as 14:00 to 14:59 UTC. A mitigation went in at 14:41 UTC, and at 15:00 UTC Anthropic identified a second issue affecting sign-in, new chats, voice and file uploads.

Anthropic notes that some messages sent during the window may not have been saved. The incident was marked resolved at 16:27 UTC.
