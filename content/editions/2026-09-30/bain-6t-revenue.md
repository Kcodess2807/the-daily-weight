A Bain & Company report estimates that AI infrastructure spending will reach about $1.5 trillion a year by 2031. To justify that, the industry would need $6 trillion in annual revenue. Bain splits it into $4.2 trillion from new products such as search, advertising and physical AI, $1 trillion to $1.4 trillion from enterprise productivity, and $200 billion to $400 billion from consumer services.

David Crawford, chairman of Bain's global technology practice, expects "a wave of innovation that will dwarf what mobile and cloud unlocked." The HN thread, near 300 comments, was mostly sceptical of the revenue side.
