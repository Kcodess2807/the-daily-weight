Dots are persistent assistants that pursue goals the user sets, working in the background alone or in teams. TechCrunch and BGR report that GPT-6 Astra powers them. They can be launched from ChatGPT or Codex, reach apps through ChatGPT's plug-ins, and report back through Slack and Teams, with text messaging "coming soon".

According to TechCrunch, specialist Dots can be given their own identities, credentials and tools, and Microsoft's Agent 365 security controls are built in. Engadget reports that each Pro tier includes one Dot and that talking to it does not count against usage limits. At launch Dots are limited to Pro and Business Premium users in eligible markets. Users on r/OpenAI reported they are not available in Europe.

The Verge frames Dots as a response to Muse, which Meta offers for free. This week Muse has been reported leaking a user's home address and ignoring permissions.
