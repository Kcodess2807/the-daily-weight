Dots are agents that take on ongoing responsibilities rather than single requests. TechCrunch reports they run on GPT-6 Astra and are available to Pro and Business Premium subscribers in eligible markets, reachable from ChatGPT, Slack and Microsoft Teams, with texting "coming soon".

Users can name a Dot, group Dots into teams, and set up specialist Dots with their own credentials and tools. Engadget reports that conversations with a Dot do not count against Pro usage limits.

OpenAI published a companion post on how it builds safety, security and privacy into Dots. For anyone designing agent permissions, that post is as relevant as the launch itself.
