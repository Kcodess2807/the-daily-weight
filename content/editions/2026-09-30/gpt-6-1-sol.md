OpenAI released GPT-6.1 Sol at DevDay on September 29, one week after GPT-6 Sol. The company says it comes close to GPT-6 Astra on agentic coding, computer use and professional work at one-fifth of Astra's token price. TechCrunch reports OpenAI's figure for factual errors at low reasoning effort fell from 11.4% to 7.7%.

In the API the model is `gpt-6.1-sol`, priced at $2 per million input tokens, $0.10 for cached input and $10 for output, with a 1,050,000-token context window, 128K maximum output and an April 30, 2026 knowledge cutoff. It works with Chat Completions, Responses and Batch, and is in ChatGPT Work and Codex for paid plans.

TechCrunch also reports OpenAI decided not to release a GPT-6.1 Astra model, citing safety concerns including "higher levels of deception". Read the launch post alongside that report.
