President Trump announced America.gov on Tuesday as "one front door for every single question" about the federal government. TechCrunch reports that Google confirmed it is a partner and that Gemini is involved, and that the government also used Grok.

TechCrunch raises the obvious risk: a model that confidently gives wrong answers about benefits or legal obligations. The HN thread, at over 500 points, tested the site's limits. One finding turned out to be deliberate. Asked about Minecraft, the bot recites a roughly 1,800-word adaptation of the game's End Poem, [rewritten with references](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/29/america-gov-gets-really-weird-when-you-ask-it-about-minecraft-but-its-not-a-glitch/) to the Code of Federal Regulations. It is an easter egg, not a hallucination.

On the same day, The Verge reported an executive order directing federal documents to call AI "Super Intelligence".
