The Electronic Frontier Foundation argues that DraftKings trains a machine learning model to find gamblers who are losing and then targets them with promotions to bring them back. It calls this "capitalizing on their vulnerability for profit instead of mitigating their risk."

The evidence is New York Times reporting from September 19, not a filing or lawsuit. The EFF uses the case to renew its call for a ban on online behavioural advertising. The post is dated September 24, but it reached HN's front page on September 29 and drew about 400 comments.
