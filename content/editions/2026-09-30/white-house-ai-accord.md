On September 29, Dario Amodei, Greg Brockman, Sundar Pichai, Mark Zuckerberg, Elon Musk and Jensen Huang signed the "Joint Commitment on Frontier Responsibilities" at the White House. The signatories commit to "robust" monitoring controls, internal oversight teams, independent external auditors and regular meetings on standards.

The accord is voluntary and names no auditor. "What other trillion-dollar industry marks its own homework?" said Toby Walsh, an AI researcher quoted by Al Jazeera.
